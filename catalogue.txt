## 2. Global rules

### Tier definitions

| Tier | Vehicle | Stays | Meals | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | Private air-conditioned car | Family-run guesthouse or budget hotel, breakfast | Breakfast only | Simple, friendly, great value |
| **Comfort** | Private A/C car or SUV (van for larger groups) | 3–4★ hotel or boutique lodge, breakfast | Breakfast + some dinners or lunches noted per plan | Balanced, most popular |
| **Signature** | Premium SUV or van, dedicated chauffeur-guide | Boutique or luxury hotel, breakfast | Half board, plus special experiences noted per plan | Refined, private, unhurried |

### Common inclusions (all packages unless noted)
- Private air-conditioned vehicle with an English-speaking chauffeur-guide for the whole itinerary
- Fuel, highway tolls, parking, driver meals and accommodation
- Airport or Colombo pickup on the first day and drop-off on the last day for multi-day plans
- Accommodation as per the chosen tier, on twin-share basis with breakfast
- Entrance fees for the main sites listed under "Extra inclusions" for each plan
- Government taxes and service charges at hotels
- Bottled water in the vehicle
- 24-hour WhatsApp support from the Serendib Days team

### Common exclusions
- International flights and travel insurance
- Meals not mentioned, drinks and personal expenses
- Entrance fees and activities not listed
- Camera or video permit fees at some sites
- Tips (drivers and guides are usually tipped about –10 per day, given at the end of the trip)
- Peak-season hotel supplements (Dec–Apr and Jul–Aug)
- Single room supplement

### Seasonal notes
- Best for the west coast, south coast and hill country: December to March.
- East coast: April to September.
- Whale watching off Mirissa: roughly November to April.
- Elephant gathering at Minneriya: roughly June to October (dry season).
- Yala National Park usually closes for part of September and October; confirm the dates each year. Offer Udawalawe or Lunugamwehera as alternatives.
- Kandy Esala Perahera: July or August.
- Horton Plains: best early morning, and clearest January to March.
- Sigiriya and Horton Plains are best done at sunrise to avoid heat and cloud.
