## Sigiriya & Dambulla Day
**Duration:** 1 Days | **Best Time:** Year-round; start early to beat the heat

**Route:** Sigiriya area → Sigiriya Rock → Dambulla Cave Temple → village lunch

**Description:**
Two of Sri Lanka's great UNESCO sites in one day: start early to climb the sky-high Lion Rock at Sigiriya in the cool morning, followed by the ancient painted caves of Dambulla, finished with a home-cooked village lunch.

### Pricing Tiers
- **ESSENTIAL**: $5 (Stays: car and guide, entrance fees, lunch not included)
- **COMFORT**: $15 (Stays: SUV, village lunch and cooking demo included)
- **SIGNATURE**: $75 (Stays: premium vehicle, private lunch at a heritage lodge, bullock-cart ride and sunset village walk)

### Itinerary
**Day 1: Rock, Caves and Village**
Early pickup from your hotel in the Sigiriya, Dambulla or Habarana area. Climb Sigiriya Rock Fortress in the cool morning to beat the heat, admiring the mirror wall and ancient frescoes. Afterwards, visit the Dambulla Cave Temple with its five caves of Buddha statues and frescoes. Lunch in a nearby village with a short cooking demonstration. Drop-off at your hotel. *(Overnight: Check route)*

## 5-Day Sri Lanka Highlights Tour
**Duration:** 5 Days | **Best Time:** Year-round

**Route:** Airport → Colombo → Galle → Udawalawe → Haputale → Nuwara Eliya → Kandy → Airport

**Description:**
Experience the highlights of Sri Lanka on an unforgettable 5-day journey through vibrant cities, historic heritage, incredible wildlife, misty mountains, scenic train rides and the world-famous Ceylon Tea.

### Pricing Tiers
- **ESSENTIAL**: $20 (Stays: Guesthouses, budget city hotels)
- **COMFORT**: $80 (Stays: 3–4★ hotels, boutique lodges)
- **SIGNATURE**: $350 (Stays: Boutique hotels, luxury resorts)

### Itinerary
**Day 1: Arrival, Colombo & Historic Galle**
Airport pickup and Colombo city tour. Continue to Galle and visit the historic Galle Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. *(Overnight: Galle)*

**Day 2: Galle Heritage & Udawalawe Wildlife**
Visit the Elephant Transit Home in Udawalawe and learn about elephant conservation. Continue to the beautiful hill country of Haputale. *(Overnight: Haputale)*

**Day 3: Scenic Train Journey & Nuwara Eliya**
Enjoy a memorable train journey from Haputale to Nanu Oya. Explore Nuwara Eliya town, known for its cool climate and colonial atmosphere. *(Overnight: Nuwara Eliya)*

**Day 4: Kandy: Nature, Culture & Tradition**
Explore the Royal Botanic Gardens, discover a Gem Factory, visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, and enjoy a traditional cultural dance. *(Overnight: Kandy)*

**Day 5: Ceylon Tea, Spices & Departure**
Visit a traditional tea factory and a spice garden. Continue to the airport for your departure. *(Overnight: Airport)*
