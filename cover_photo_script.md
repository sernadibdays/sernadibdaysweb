# Image Generation Prompts for Serendib Days Packages

This document contains optimized prompts to generate stunning, photorealistic cover images for each of your 24 packages. You can copy and paste these prompts into Gemini Pro (Imagen 3), Midjourney, or DALL-E 3.

**General Style Notes applied to all prompts:**
* Photorealistic, 8k resolution, highly detailed, stunning lighting.
* No text, no logos, no watermarks.
* Cinematic composition, travel photography style.

---

## Package 1: Sigiriya & Dambulla Day
**Route:** Sigiriya area → Sigiriya Rock → Dambulla Cave Temple → village lunch
**Interests:** Culture & Heritage, Adventure

**Prompt:**
> A breathtaking, photorealistic travel photography shot of Sri Lanka. An awe-inspiring shot of an ancient Sri Lankan temple or ruin representing Sigiriya area → Sigiriya Rock → Dambulla Cave Temple → village lunch. Intricate stone carvings, monks in orange robes, peaceful and spiritual atmosphere, warm glowing sunlight. High quality, 8k, photorealistic, taken with a DSLR, no text.
